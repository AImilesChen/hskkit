# HSKKit PRD

## Positioning

HSKKit is an English-market Chinese learning tool site for HSK learners. It helps learners generate practical study materials with AI.

## P1 scope

- AI worksheet generator
- AI graded reading generator
- HSK flashcards
- Free usage limits
- Pro subscription with monthly AI credits
- Legal pages and support contact

## Pricing and limits

- Payment currency: USD
- Payment provider: PayPal
- Pro monthly: $5.99/month
- Pro yearly: $49/year
- Free: Worksheet 1/day, Reading 2 of 5, Flashcards first 30
- Pro: 500 credits/month, reset monthly, no rollover
- AI costs: Worksheet 2 credits, Reading 20 credits, Flashcards 5 credits
- Refund: 7 days
- Failed payment grace: 3 days
- Support: support@hskkit.com

## Launch requirements

- Landing page
- Pricing section
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Sitemap and robots
- Evidence checklist before hard launch
