## 1. 5 篇 HSK 1 原创 Reading with Pinyin

### Reading 01: My Family

**Title (EN):** My Family
**Title (CN):** 我的家
**Target Vocabulary (8 words):** 家, 爸爸, 妈妈, 儿子, 女儿, 爱, 有, 个

**Chinese Content:**
我的家有三个人。爸爸、妈妈和我。爸爸是老师，妈妈也是老师。他们很爱我。我有一个小狗，它叫小白。小白很喜欢我。我们每天一起吃饭，一起看电视。我爱我的家。

**Pinyin Content:**
Wǒ de jiā yǒu sān gè rén. Bàba, māma hé wǒ. Bàba shì lǎoshī, māma yě shì lǎoshī. Tāmen hěn ài wǒ. Wǒ yǒu yī zhī xiǎo gǒu, tā jiào Xiǎo Bái. Xiǎo Bái hěn xǐhuan wǒ. Wǒmen měitiān yīqǐ chī fàn, yīqǐ kàn diànshì. Wǒ ài wǒ de jiā.

**English Summary:**
This is a short story about a family of three. Both parents are teachers. The narrator has a small dog named Xiaobai. They eat and watch TV together every day.

**Highlighted Vocabulary (with pinyin & meaning):**
- 家 (jiā) — home; family
- 爸爸 (bàba) — father; dad
- 妈妈 (māma) — mother; mom
- 儿子 (érzi) — son
- 爱 (ài) — to love
- 有 (yǒu) — to have
- 个 (gè) — a (general measure word)
- 很 (hěn) — very

**Comprehension Questions:**
1. Q: 家有几个人？ / How many people are in the family?
   A: 三个人。 / Three people.
2. Q: 爸爸和妈妈是什么工作？ / What do the father and mother do?
   A: 他们是老师。 / They are teachers.
3. Q: 小狗叫什么名字？ / What is the dog's name?
   A: 小白。 / Xiaobai.

**Study Tip:**
Notice how 很 (hěn) is used before adjectives like 爱 and 喜欢. In HSK 1, 很 often functions as a softener rather than meaning "very" in a strong sense.

### Reading 02: A Day at School

**Title (EN):** A Day at School
**Title (CN):** 在学校的一天
**Target Vocabulary (8 words):** 学校, 老师, 学生, 同学, 书, 读, 学习, 汉字

**Chinese Content:**
我是学生，我在北京学习汉语。我的学校不大，但是很好。老师每天教我们汉字。我们有五本书。我喜欢读书，我的同学也喜欢。下午三点，我们回家。明天再见，老师！

**Pinyin Content:**
Wǒ shì xuésheng, wǒ zài Běijīng xuéxí Hànyǔ. Wǒ de xuéxiào bù dà, dànshì hěn hǎo. Lǎoshī měitiān jiāo wǒmen Hànzì. Wǒmen yǒu wǔ běn shū. Wǒ xǐhuan dú shū, wǒ de tóngxué yě xǐhuan. Xiàwǔ sān diǎn, wǒmen huí jiā. Míngtiān zàijiàn, lǎoshī!

**English Summary:**
A student studies Chinese in Beijing. The school is small but good. The teacher teaches Chinese characters every day. The student and classmates like reading books. They go home at 3 PM.

**Highlighted Vocabulary:**
- 学校 (xuéxiào) — school
- 老师 (lǎoshī) — teacher
- 学生 (xuésheng) — student
- 同学 (tóngxué) — classmate
- 书 (shū) — book
- 读 (dú) — to read
- 学习 (xuéxí) — to study; to learn
- 汉字 (Hànzì) — Chinese characters

**Comprehension Questions:**
1. Q: 我在哪里学习汉语？ / Where do I study Chinese?
   A: 在北京。 / In Beijing.
2. Q: 老师教什么？ / What does the teacher teach?
   A: 汉字。 / Chinese characters.
3. Q: 我们几点回家？ / What time do we go home?
   A: 下午三点。 / 3 PM.

**Study Tip:**
Pay attention to the measure word 本 (běn) for books. "五本书" means "five books." Measure words are essential in Chinese counting.

### Reading 03: Going to the Restaurant

**Title (EN):** Going to the Restaurant
**Title (CN):** 去饭店吃饭
**Target Vocabulary (8 words):** 饭店, 吃, 饭, 菜, 茶, 水, 杯子, 好吃

**Chinese Content:**
今天中午，我和朋友去饭店吃饭。饭店不大，但是菜很好吃。我们吃了米饭，喝了茶。服务员给了我们四个杯子。一杯是水，三杯是茶。朋友说：“这个菜很好吃！”我也说：“是，很好吃！”

**Pinyin Content:**
Jīntiān zhōngwǔ, wǒ hé péngyou qù fàndiàn chī fàn. Fàndiàn bù dà, dànshì cài hěn hǎo chī. Wǒmen chī le mǐfàn, hē le chá. Fúwùyuán gěi le wǒmen sì gè bēizi. Yī bēi shì shuǐ, sān bēi shì chá. Péngyou shuō: "Zhège cài hěn hǎo chī!" Wǒ yě shuō: "Shì, hěn hǎo chī!"

**English Summary:**
At noon, the narrator and a friend go to a restaurant. The dishes are delicious. They eat rice and drink tea. The waiter gives them four cups — one water and three teas. Both agree the food is tasty.

**Highlighted Vocabulary:**
- 饭店 (fàndiàn) — restaurant
- 吃 (chī) — to eat
- 饭 (fàn) — rice; meal
- 菜 (cài) — dish; vegetable
- 茶 (chá) — tea
- 水 (shuǐ) — water
- 杯子 (bēizi) — cup; glass
- 好吃 (hǎo chī) — delicious

**Comprehension Questions:**
1. Q: 我们什么时候去饭店？ / When do we go to the restaurant?
   A: 今天中午。 / Today at noon.
2. Q: 我们吃了什么？ / What did we eat?
   A: 米饭。 / Rice.
3. Q: 有几个杯子？ / How many cups are there?
   A: 四个。 / Four.

**Study Tip:**
The word 了 (le) appears after verbs to show completed action: "吃了米饭" = "ate rice." This is one of the most common grammar points in HSK 1.

### Reading 04: A Trip to Beijing

**Title (EN):** A Trip to Beijing
**Title (CN):** 去北京
**Target Vocabulary (8 words):** 北京, 飞机, 出租车, 酒店, 天气, 冷, 高兴, 好看

**Chinese Content:**
明天我去北京。我坐飞机去，很快。到了北京，我坐出租车去酒店。北京的天气很冷，但是我很高兴。我看见了很多好看的东西。我想买一些东西给我的妈妈。北京很大，我很喜欢！

**Pinyin Content:**
Míngtiān wǒ qù Běijīng. Wǒ zuò fēijī qù, hěn kuài. Dào le Běijīng, wǒ zuò chūzūchē qù jiǔdiàn. Běijīng de tiānqì hěn lěng, dànshì wǒ hěn gāoxìng. Wǒ kànjiàn le hěn duō hǎokàn de dōngxi. Wǒ xiǎng mǎi yīxiē dōngxi gěi wǒ de māma. Běijīng hěn dà, wǒ hěn xǐhuan!

**English Summary:**
The narrator is traveling to Beijing tomorrow by plane. After arriving, they take a taxi to a hotel. The weather is cold, but they are happy. They see many beautiful things and want to buy something for their mother.

**Highlighted Vocabulary:**
- 北京 (Běijīng) — Beijing
- 飞机 (fēijī) — airplane
- 出租车 (chūzūchē) — taxi
- 酒店 (jiǔdiàn) — hotel
- 天气 (tiānqì) — weather
- 冷 (lěng) — cold
- 高兴 (gāoxìng) — happy; glad
- 好看 (hǎokàn) — good-looking; beautiful

**Comprehension Questions:**
1. Q: 我怎么去北京？ / How do I go to Beijing?
   A: 坐飞机。 / By plane.
2. Q: 北京的天气怎么样？ / How is the weather in Beijing?
   A: 很冷。 / Very cold.
3. Q: 我想给谁买东西？ / Who do I want to buy things for?
   A: 妈妈。 / Mother.

**Study Tip:**
The pattern "给 + someone + verb" means "to do something for someone." Here, "给妈妈买东西" means "buy things for mother." This is a very useful HSK 1 structure.

### Reading 05: My Favorite Things

**Title (EN):** My Favorite Things
**Title (CN):** 我喜欢的东西
**Target Vocabulary (8 words):** 喜欢, 猫, 狗, 电影, 电脑, 咖啡, 音乐, 朋友

**Chinese Content:**
我喜欢很多东西。我喜欢猫，我也喜欢狗。我家有一只猫，它叫小花。我喜欢看电影，每个星期看一次。我也喜欢听音乐，用电脑听。早上我喜欢喝咖啡。下午我喜欢和朋友一起玩。你最喜欢什么？

**Pinyin Content:**
Wǒ xǐhuan hěn duō dōngxi. Wǒ xǐhuan māo, wǒ yě xǐhuan gǒu. Wǒ jiā yǒu yī zhī māo, tā jiào Xiǎo Huā. Wǒ xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng, měi gè xīngqī kàn yī cì. Wǒ yě xǐhuan tīng yīnyuè, yòng diànnǎo tīng. Zǎoshang wǒ xǐhuan hē kāfēi. Xiàwǔ wǒ xǐhuan hé péngyou yīqǐ wán. Nǐ zuì xǐhuan shénme?

**English Summary:**
The narrator likes many things — cats, dogs, movies, music, coffee, and friends. They have a cat named Xiaohua. They watch a movie once a week and listen to music on the computer. They end by asking the reader: what do you like most?

**Highlighted Vocabulary:**
- 喜欢 (xǐhuan) — to like
- 猫 (māo) — cat
- 狗 (gǒu) — dog
- 电影 (diànyǐng) — movie; film
- 电脑 (diànnǎo) — computer
- 咖啡 (kāfēi) — coffee
- 音乐 (yīnyuè) — music
- 朋友 (péngyou) — friend

**Comprehension Questions:**
1. Q: 我家的猫叫什么名字？ / What is my cat's name?
   A: 小花。 / Xiaohua.
2. Q: 我什么时候看电影？ / When do I watch movies?
   A: 每个星期一次。 / Once a week.
3. Q: 我用什么听音乐？ / What do I use to listen to music?
   A: 电脑。 / Computer.

**Study Tip:**
The word 最 (zuì) means "most." "你最喜欢什么？" = "What do you like most?" This is a great question pattern to practice with classmates or language partners.

### FAQ / Schema 文案

**Q1: Is this practice sheet generator free?**
A: Yes. You can generate and print practice sheets without creating an account. A daily soft limit may apply to prevent abuse.

**Q2: What is Tian Zi Ge (田字格)?**
A: Tian Zi Ge is a traditional Chinese writing grid with a cross in the middle. It helps learners balance character strokes and maintain proper proportions.

**Q3: Can I download a PDF instead of printing?**
A: Currently, the generator outputs a browser-optimized print page. You can "Print to PDF" using your browser's built-in feature. A dedicated PDF download option is planned for a future release.

**Q4: Is there a limit to how many characters I can enter?**
A: For best print results, we recommend 5–10 characters per sheet. Longer inputs may be split across multiple pages automatically.

**Q5: Can teachers use this for their classes?**
A: Yes. Teachers can generate unlimited sheets for classroom use. A Teacher Pack with batch generation and custom templates will be available in a future release.
