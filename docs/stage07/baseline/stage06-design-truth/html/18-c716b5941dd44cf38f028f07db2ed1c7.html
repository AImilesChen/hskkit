The "ba" construction (把)

The 把 (bǎ) structure is one of the most important grammatical concepts in Chinese. It is used to highlight the object and the result of an action.

Explanation & Structure

In standard English or basic Chinese syntax, sentences typically follow a Subject + Verb + Object (SVO) pattern. The 把 (bǎ) sentence pulls the object forward, placing it immediately after the subject and before the verb.

Subject + 把 + Object + Verb + Result/Direction

Crucial Note: You cannot simply end a 把 sentence with just a verb. There must be some "disposal" or change of state affecting the object (the Result/Direction part).

Example Sentences

1
我把书放在桌子上了。
Wǒ bǎ shū fàng zài zhuōzi shang le.
I put the book on the table.
2
请把门关上。
Qǐng bǎ mén guānshang.
Please close the door.
3
他把我的咖啡喝完了。
Tā bǎ wǒ de kāfēi hē wán le.
He drank (finished) my coffee.
