Vocabulary chevron_right HSK 1 chevron_right 你好
HSK 1 Noun / Phrase
nǐ hǎo

你好

PRIMARY MEANING

Hello; How do you do?

Usage Examples

  • 你好，很高兴认识你。
    Nǐ hǎo, hěn gāoxìng rènshì nǐ.
    Hello, nice to meet you.
  • 李老师，你好！
    Lǐ lǎoshī, nǐ hǎo!
    Hello, Teacher Li!

Character Structure

你
nǐ - you
Radical: 亻 (person)
好
hǎo - good
Radical: 女 (woman) + 子 (child)
