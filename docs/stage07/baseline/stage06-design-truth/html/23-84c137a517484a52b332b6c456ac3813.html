Understand the HSK 3.0 learning framework

The new HSK standard expands from 6 levels to a comprehensive 9-level system, providing a more granular and rigorous path to Chinese language proficiency.

Three Stages, Nine Levels

layers

Beginner

Level 1 Level 2 Level 3

Focuses on foundational phonetics, basic characters, and daily survival communication. Requires mastery of ~2,245 words total by Level 3.

translate

Intermediate

Level 4 Level 5 Level 6

Enables fluid conversation across varied topics. Emphasizes complex grammar and longer reading comprehension. ~5,456 cumulative words.

school

Advanced

Level 7 Level 8 Level 9

Designed for academic and professional fluency. Translation, cultural nuance, and specialized vocabulary. Exceeds 11,000 words.
