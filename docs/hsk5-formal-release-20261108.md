# HSK5 accepted A/B/C mock papers

Scope: publish the three user-accepted HSK5 papers at `/hsk-test-prep/hsk-5/mock`. Paper A is free; B/C use the existing Pro subscription, trial or pass. Prices, payment flows, HSK1–4 papers, database schema and schedules are unchanged. Existing cache cleanup remains disabled.

Each paper has 72 tasks: 35 listening, 35 reading and two manual writing tasks. The recording lasts 25 minutes, with each dialogue or passage read once. Reading includes cloze passages, two five-paragraph ordering groups and comprehension. The four-picture story requires at least 100 Chinese characters; the topic essay requires at least 200. Allow five minutes to prepare before starting the server-timed 105-minute attempt: listening25, answer transfer5, reading35 and writing40.

Selected choices save immediately. Both writing drafts save explicitly, support newlines, restore on return, and may be saved below the minimum. The displayed count excludes punctuation, Latin letters and digits. Failed saves expose a retry, the original deadline persists on return, and expiry submits saved answers. Navigation uses the actual eight groups and four consecutive story pictures. Shared reading text and paragraph banks remain available.

Results report listening and reading raw marks out of35 each, with70 objective marks total. Both writing responses are labelled Self-review, including exact matches to references. The story uses a separate10-point editorial rubric and the essay a separate20-point rubric; these are never combined into an official score, scaled score or pass prediction.

Runtime data and protected media are bound to the accepted paper/audio hashes. Student views exclude scored answers, scripts, explanations and reference responses. B/C access is enforced before streaming, including Range requests. Direct static paths remain denied. No production database fixtures, clock changes or new tasks are included. Production verification and rollback evidence are recorded after actual deployment.
