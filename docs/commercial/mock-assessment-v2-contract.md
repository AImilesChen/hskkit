# HSK Practice Assessment Pack v2 — frozen delivery contract

Status: approved by product owner on 2026-08-26

## Positioning

- Keep the product positioned as an **unofficial HSK 3.0 level-aligned practice assessment / mock practice pack**.
- Never claim official HSK authorship, an official blueprint, an official score, or equivalence to an official examination.
- Levels 7–9 must continue to disclose that they share the unified official advanced-band canonical source set.

## Required deliverables

The ZIP must include:

1. Three distinct student forms: A, B and C.
2. One real MP3 listening track for each form.
3. Separate answer sheets suitable for printing.
4. Answers with item explanations and distractor analysis.
5. Facilitator/listening transcripts with playback and pause instructions.
6. Level-specific scoring rubrics.
7. A learner score report and a weakness-to-practice recommendation page.
8. A score-tracker CSV.
9. README and manifest files.

## Level differentiation

- Levels 1–2: short utterances/dialogues, sentence meaning, information matching, basic word order and short controlled output.
- Levels 3–4: dialogues, cloze, sentence ordering, information matching, short-passage comprehension and guided writing.
- Levels 5–6: longer dialogues/passages, paragraph cloze, detail/inference, sentence insertion, summary and structured writing.
- Levels 7–9: extended discourse, inference, multi-source information, paragraph organization, synthesis, summary and argument tasks.
- Each level must have an explicit suggested time and a visibly different task mix.

## Answer and scoring quality

- Every objective item has the correct answer, a concise explanation and distractor analysis.
- Writing rubrics must be differentiated by level band rather than reusing one 2/1/0 rule for Levels 1–9.
- Score reports must identify the weakest skill and map it to specific recommended practice.
- Diagnostic results must be labelled unofficial and must not be described as an official HSK score.

## Audio

- MP3 files must contain intelligible Mandarin speech, not silence or placeholder tones.
- The student forms must point to the correct form audio.
- Transcripts must state playback count, natural pace, pauses and response time.
- Generated voice provenance and permitted product use must be documented without embedding credentials or provider secrets.

## Layout

- Provide candidate name/date/level fields and a separate printable answer sheet.
- Do not split one question across pages.
- Writing tasks require usable response space.
- Supply A4 print-safe pages with readable body type and low-ink styling.
- No orphan tail pages or unexplained large blank areas.
