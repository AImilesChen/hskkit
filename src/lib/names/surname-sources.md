editorial starting collection, not a frequency ranking or a claim of complete
coverage. Readings are name-use Mandarin; regional/family readings can differ.
Original compact records are maintained in surnames.ts, not scraped entries.

Surname readings are stored per surname and per syllable, including 曾 Zēng,
任 Rén, 沈 Shěn, 贾 Jiǎ, 宁 Nìng, 长孙 Zhǎngsūn, 尉迟 Yùchí and 令狐 Línghú. Do not
replace these with a character's most common dictionary reading. Audio is
machine-synthesized; complete native-speaker listening review is not claimed.

For selected polyphonic readings, the offline synthesis manifest records an
explicit homophonic synthesis spelling separately from the displayed transcript.
Both scripts and their hashes are retained; the displayed surname is unchanged.
