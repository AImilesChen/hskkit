Create a polished 16:9 editorial scene illustration for an evidence-based Chinese-learning article.

SUBJECT
an adult learner closing a reference card and recalling a Chinese character into a square-grid notebook, with a few component cards and word-context cards nearby.

COMPOSITION
One strong focal point, spacious but information-rich horizontal composition, learner and study materials grouped by proximity, clear left-to-right visual flow, balanced alignment, foreground and background depth. Keep important subjects away from extreme edges so the image crops well on desktop and mobile cards.

STYLE
Modern editorial illustration, believable learning scene, refined vector-and-gouache feel, subtle paper texture, natural human proportions, calm premium education brand, culturally respectful, visually distinctive but not childish, no generic 3D icons, no glassmorphism, no stock-photo look.

COLORS
Deep navy and cobalt blue as dominant colors, cyan highlights, warm off-white background, one restrained coral accent. Strong contrast at the focal action, softer contrast in supporting details.

CONTENT RULES
No logos, no watermark, no app interface, no exam certificate, no flags, no mascot. Avoid legible English or Chinese paragraphs; any paper markings should be abstract or use only a few clearly intentional learning symbols. Do not invent scores, statistics, or product claims.

OUTPUT
