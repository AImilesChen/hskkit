Frequently Asked Questions

Is HSKKit free to use?

Yes. The Free plan includes the full HSK Level Test, HSK 1 vocabulary browsing, one practice test per day, 2 of 5 reading articles, one browser-print worksheet per day, and the first 30 flashcard words. No credit card required.

Do I need to create an account?

You can take the level test and do basic practice without signing up. To save progress across devices and access Pro features, you'll need a free account.

What are AI credits and how do they work?

Pro plans include 500 AI credits per month. Each AI Explanation uses 2 credits. Each AI Study Plan uses 20 credits. Each AI Quiz Generation uses 5 credits per 10-question set. Credits reset at the start of each billing cycle and do not roll over. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. Cancel primarily through your PayPal subscription management. If you need help, email support@hskkit.com. After cancellation, Pro access remains active until the end of your current billing period.

What happens if my payment fails?

You get a 3-day grace period to update your payment method. During this time, Pro access continues. After the grace period, your account downgrades to Free automatically.

Can I get a refund?

You may request a refund within 7 days of your initial purchase. Refunds may be denied or prorated if AI credits were heavily used. After 7 days, payments are generally non-refundable, except where required by applicable law.

Is HSKKit an official HSK prep tool?

No. HSKKit is an independent learning tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any official HSK test organizer. AI-generated explanations and study plans are for educational purposes only and do not guarantee exam results.

What makes HSKKit different from Duolingo or ChineseSkill?

HSKKit is built specifically for HSK exam preparation — not general language learning. We focus on test-style questions, reading with pinyin, printable worksheets, and a 10-minute daily loop. We also cost less than most competitors.

Can I use HSKKit for my students or children?

Yes. Parents and teachers can use HSKKit on their own accounts to assist children or students with learning.

Is my data safe?

We use industry-standard security measures. We do not store full payment details on our servers — PayPal handles all payment processing. See our Privacy Policy for details on data collection and retention.
